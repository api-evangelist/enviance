Enviance (now Cority Enviance) is an environmental, health, and safety (EHS) compliance-management SaaS platform. Originally an independent cloud environmental-compliance vendor, Enviance was acquired by Cority and its product now ships as "Cority Enviance." The platform manages regulatory requirements, compliance tasks, emissions and environmental events, chemicals and materials inventories, documents, workflows, and reporting for enterprises in regulated industries. Its EMS REST API (the "Cority Enviance App API") exposes 250+ operations across 39 service areas — Activity, Chemical, Material, Compliance, Requirement, Event, Task, Workflow, Document, Report, Portal, Bulk data, SCIM identity, and more — for building custom interfaces and integrating Enviance with corporate and third-party systems.

**Areas:** Company · Environmental · Compliance · EHS · Sustainability · Chemical Management · Regulatory · Software-as-a-Service

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Enviance: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
